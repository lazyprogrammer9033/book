Privacy

Kirti's Beauty Parlour uses its own salon app to manage appointments, customers and receipts.

When the salon owner connects Google Drive or Google Calendar, the app only uses that access to save the salon's own backup files and appointment events in the owner's account. Nothing is shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.

Customer details you give when booking (name, phone, email) are used only to manage your appointment and send you confirmations and reminders.

Questions: please contact the salon directly.
